FAQ

The questions you’re already asking.

What kind of projects do you work on?

Web platforms, mobile apps, and applied AI — from a new product built from zero, to adding automation on top of something you already run. If it involves software, we can probably help.

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp or email with a short description of what you’re building. We’ll reply with clarifying questions, not a sales pitch, and can usually scope the first steps within a few days.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, timeline, and complexity. Tell us what you’re building and we’ll give you a clear estimate before any work starts — no surprise invoices.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused MVP can ship in a few weeks; larger platforms take longer. We break work into visible milestones so you always know where things stand.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We can hand everything over for your team to own, stay on for ongoing maintenance and features, or somewhere in between — whatever fits how you work.

Can you work with our existing team or codebase?

Yes. We regularly plug into existing repos, tech stacks, and in-house teams, and can work alongside your engineers rather than replacing them.
