FAQ

The questions you’re already asking.

What does a normal day look like?

You share priorities and context; your agentic engineer directs the fleet and reports back with shipped work. No stand-up overhead, no managing a team of contractors.

How do I see progress?

Every change lands in your repository as reviewed pull requests, plus a live activity view. Nothing is a black box.

Is the code any good?

All output passes automated review and test agents before it reaches you, and a human engineer owns the final call. You get production-grade code, not prototypes.

What happens when the engagement ends?

You own everything — the code, the repo, the history. No proprietary runtime, no lock-in. Scale the engagement up or down as you need.

Can you work in a regulated environment?

Yes. Review and governance agents enforce compliance-aware delivery, with a full audit trail of every change.
